## Learning / regression plane

Prometeo now distinguishes these durable semantic artifact classes:

- **INCIDENT**: immutable observed expectation-vs-reality evidence.
- **NEGATIVE_PHENOTYPE**: reusable trigger + observable signature + violated invariant; root-cause hypotheses may evolve separately.
- **VACCINE**: scoped prevention/detection/recovery primitive.
- **GOLDEN**: bounded verified positive reference behavior/artifact.
- **FALSIFICATION_CASE**: executable/replayable case that distinguishes intact protection from breakage/removal.
- **REGRESSION_SUITE**: compiled task/scope-specific projection selecting applicable Goldens, Vaccines and Falsifiers.

Promotion law:
- One success is not a Golden.
- One failure is not a global Vaccine.
- A prevention cannot become ACTIVE without scope, evidence, before/after or equivalent causal support, preserve/regression checks, a negative-control/falsifier, owning integration receipt and rollback/disable path.
- A Golden cannot imply Current/Human Accepted/Served.
- Every promoted prevention must have a falsifier capable of detecting removal/breakage.
- Recurrence after an ACTIVE Vaccine reopens the incident/phenotype/prevention model; the system must not hide the escape behind the old ACTIVE label.
