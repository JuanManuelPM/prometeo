01
Chapter 01 · Arrival

The room changes first.

A cat enters a space by measuring it. Doorway, chair leg, shadow, human ankle: each object is tested for distance and consequence before it becomes ordinary.

That small delay is useful. Attention can be active without being loud. The cat is building a map by moving through the room.

Watch the pauses. They are not empty; they are where the next movement is chosen.

02
Chapter 02 · Signal

Most language is position.

Ears rotate before the body follows. A tail changes tempo. Weight shifts toward a doorway or away from a hand.

Reading a cat means noticing changes, not memorizing a dictionary of poses. The same gesture changes meaning with context.

Context turns movement into information.

03
Chapter 03 · Territory

A path is a remembered decision.

Windowsills, table edges and the back of a sofa become repeated routes: some for speed, some for surveillance, some for protection.

Territory is not only ownership. It is predictability: the next foothold is already known.

Good environments offer more than one safe way through.

04
Chapter 04 · Rest

Stillness has structure.

A resting cat still chooses: warm surface, protected edge, clear exit, familiar scent. Comfort is assembled from small conditions.

Rest is not the absence of activity; it is the result of enough signals saying action can stop for now.

When the conditions are right, the body believes them.
