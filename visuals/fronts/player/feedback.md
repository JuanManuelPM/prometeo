# 🖐️ Retro Player / Hand Loops · Durable Feedback

Status: CURRENT

## KEEP / ACCEPTED
- persistent scene/state
- low internal resolution
- touch
- cheap audio/vibration
- image-first foreground

## OPEN / NEXT REVIEW
- A few static hand photos are not enough.
- Idle should loop roughly 8–12 hand images.
- Actions such as grab should be multi-image sequences.
- Abrupt collage cuts are acceptable and may be desirable.
- Reference Curator should eventually provide tagged hand collections by action.
- Action should leave persistent scene consequences where useful.

## REJECTED / DO NOT REVIVE
- One nearly invisible dark hand.
- Two/four static poses treated as a finished animation.
- Primitive-drawn hands.
