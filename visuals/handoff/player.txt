WHY V27
V26 proved pointer-follow works but user feedback identified:
- default pupil too small;
- several eye modes moved only in a small central radius;
- side glances should use the entire white eye and may show only part of the pupil;
- touching/clicking should establish gaze first;
- color/type changes must preserve gaze;
- audio was too bass-heavy.

V27 GAZE
- default pupil radius: 0.34;
- pointer sampling: 10 FPS;
- angle quantization: 24 steps;
- radial quantization: 6 steps;
- gaze limit is not a fixed radius;
- runtime ray-casts through the real open V21 aperture mask;
- center may exceed the aperture edge by 0.58 pupil radii;
- current eyelid/aperture mask clips the rest;
- therefore extreme side glances can show only part of the pupil.

GLOBAL GAZE RULE
Pointer/touch is updated before any click action.
Color, shape and dilation states do not recenter gaze.

V27 CLICK SCENES
follow
- pointer follows full aperture;
- click blinks.

dilate
- starts at current gaze;
- 26 discrete steps over ~2.4 s;
- ends full black;
- next click contracts again.

color
- color changes;
- gaze stays where pointer is.

shape
- circle / oval / diamond / slit / cross / star / dot;
- gaze stays pointer-driven.

combo
- color + morphology mutation;
- gaze stays pointer-driven.

AUDIO
New deterministic 32 kHz procedural loop includes:
- kick/sub around 58-100 Hz;
- bass notes around 98-147 Hz;
- mid plucks 330-660 Hz;
- chimes 1.2-4.1 kHz;
- high clicks 4.2-5.4 kHz;
- bright noise hats.

Analyser visualization samples logarithmically from ~70 Hz to 8 kHz.
