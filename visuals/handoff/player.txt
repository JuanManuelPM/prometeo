NEW V26 POINTER SCENES

1. pointer_follow
- pupil follows mouse/touch pointer.

2. pointer_dilate
- gaze remains pointer-driven;
- click starts a 22-step dilation;
- pupil expands from current gaze position toward full black;
- final full-black state intentionally hides pupil.

3. pointer_color
- click cycles sclera colors;
- gaze remains pointer-driven;
- dark sclera switches pupil to white so tracking stays visible.

4. pointer_shape
- click cycles circle / oval / diamond / slit / cross / star / dot;
- pupil keeps following pointer.

5. pointer_combo
- click mutates both color and morphology;
- gaze remains pointer-controlled.
