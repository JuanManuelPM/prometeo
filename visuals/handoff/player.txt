WHY V25
The user wants the eye to become a reusable effect surface rather than a small fixed list of pupil modes:
- richer animations;
- infinitely variable colors through parameters;
- longer/more readable dilation;
- eye as play/pause audio control;
- audio-reactive movement lines;
- GIF/video/media inside the eye aperture;
- black/red/white transformations;
- low-FPS visual language preserved.

AUDIO PRECEDENT
Prior Prometeo audio work established:
audio source → Web Audio AnalyserNode → waveform/energy → quantized line-boil visualization.
V25 reuses this design.

V25 EFFECTS
dilate_long
- 14 discrete stages
- tiny pupil → many intermediate radii → full black

contract_red
- full black → progressively smaller centered pupil
- newly freed sclera becomes red

scan
- stepped low-FPS gaze

chromatic
- hue is parameterized
- content fps can be overridden

audio_player
- eye becomes PLAY/PAUSE
- first user interaction starts browser-local procedural audio
- click toggles AudioContext running/suspended
- AnalyserNode drives actual waveform halo
- waveform visual is quantized at low FPS

media_portal
- ?media=<url> loads GIF or video into aperture
- without media URL, procedural moving scan-field fallback is shown

glitch
- discrete color / gaze / pupil-shape jumps
