   - Fix: extend cylinder geometry far above the viewport so tops do not resolve; use crop + smooth upper darkness, not visible endpoints.
3. Cylinder roundness was reinforced with more radial segments.

CRITICAL PROGRAMMING RULE

Depth attenuation belongs to world Z:
- floor fades by floor-band depth;
- objects fade by object depth;
- no hard horizontal seam should cut through cylinders.
