LATEST LIGHTING CORRECTION
The user wants one strict physical rule:
- every sampled point is lit by its true distance from the center of the camera;
- closer = brighter;
- farther = darker;
- the base of a nearby tower should brighten strongly;
- the tip of a huge tower should be almost invisible because it is much farther away vertically.

v17 CHANGES
- cameraLight3DAt is now pure Euclidean X/Y/Z distance;
- the secondary optical-axis multiplier from v16 was removed;
- cameraLightAt is only a wrapper around the same 3D metric;
- towers use 12 vertical slices;
- slices are distributed nonlinearly with much more resolution near the base;
- curvature remains for roundness but cannot override proximity;
- floors and Map 1 lighting use the same camera-center rule.

ROOT CAUSE FIXED
