CURRENT OBJECTIVE
Move from 1–4 isolated hand poses to reusable image-sequence loops.

Required minimum:
- IDLE loop with roughly 8–12 hand images/frames;
- at least one multi-frame action sequence such as GRAB;
- preferably POINT / PUSH / ATTACK / OFFER collections too;
- abrupt collage-like cuts are allowed;
- large, high-contrast foreground hand;
- preserve persistent scene/state machine, touch, cheap audio/vibration and low internal resolution if still useful;
- action sequence should lead to a persistent scene change.

ASSET STRATEGY
Gather many hand references/assets, not the first two acceptable images. Group them by action. If Curator exists, consume its tagged collections. If not, create a local reusable hand-sequence manifest that Curator can adopt later.

REJECTION GATES
- no single dark transparent hand;
- no “four static hands” presented as final animation;
- no primitive-drawn hand;
- no generic HUD overpowering the scene;
- no smooth interpolation requirement if hard collage cuts look better.

SELF-CRITIQUE
State whether IDLE and ACTION feel alive in motion and what remains fake.
