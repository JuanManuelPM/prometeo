CURRENT OBJECTIVE
Preserve the current walking passage as baseline but push it toward brutalist, monumental, continuous architecture.

Required:
- LEFT/RIGHT = yaw/look only;
- UP/DOWN = straight forward/back;
- continuous floor under camera, never black hole;
- walls, floor and ceiling keep distinct material identities;
- remove the feeling of stacked picture frames;
- when one arch/layer leaves view, another must already continue behind/through it;
- overlap, occlude, extend or blend modules so visible edges disappear;
- make scale much larger so the viewer feels small;
- add sparse encountered objects/figures if they strengthen depth;
- preserve darkness and retro/creepy atmosphere.
